Host architecture evidence (0.2.1-rc.5, Electron 44.7.0)
-------------------------------------------------------
app.asar/lib/main.js:
  - the Electron main process spawns the Host child:
      spawn(node, [entry, runtimeDir, projectDir, ...], { stdio: [ignore, pipe, pipe, ipc] })
      entry = node_modules/@deepseek-ai/dsh-desktop-host/lib/index.js
  - the child -> main IPC switch accepts only: ready, shutdown, quit-inspection,
    update-tasks, platform-session (plus the ready/control reply shapes).
  - lib/preload-app.cjs exposes no window-creation API; the only window bridge
    is dshPlatform.open for the account usage/top-up pages.
app.asar/dsh/node_modules/@deepseek-ai/dsh-desktop-host/lib/index.js:
  - runProfile drives the dsh "desktop" profile in that child and reports the
    authenticated URL back to Electron.
Therefore a plugin cannot create or keep an Electron window of its own.
